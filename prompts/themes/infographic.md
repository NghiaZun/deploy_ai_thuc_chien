## Lock the information contract

- Define one question, one answer, and one action for a named reader; select only figures the team can verify. The final idea's wow moment must be legible **at a glance** and still accurate up close. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Coordinator records for each plotted value: source title/date, original units, calculation, rounding, and destination visual. If figures are not obtainable, pivot to a sourced qualitative process diagram; do not make up percentages to preserve the layout. [HYPOTHESIS]
- Ask for the official image/PDF dimensions, orientation, pixel resolution and submission route; until confirmed set them `UNKNOWN` with an early check. Keep source SVG/HTML or editable layout and a reopened export. [FROM USER] [HYPOTHESIS]

## Judge-lens and cuts

- [HYPOTHESIS] First impression: a true relationship jumps out immediately; fine print remains readable when inspected. Mediocre: tiny unrelated facts and a decorative AI illustration that obscures numbers.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Cut order: second chart -> decorative art -> secondary callouts. Never cut data attribution, central mark, or the viewer's action.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Before contest: prepare editable tall/wide grids, chart components and a one-page source ledger; test export legibility on a phone. Find inspiration with `editorial infographic hierarchy comparison layout` but verify reuse rights for any imported asset.
