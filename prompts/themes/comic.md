[HYPOTHESIS] Use the fewest panels that communicate the complete arc; define panel IDs, reading order, repeated character silhouette/outfit, prop and color lock. ## Fill this panel brief

```text
Make a continuity-safe {PANEL_COUNT}-panel sheet for {FINAL_IDEA_STORY}. For each panel provide: {PANEL_ID}, one visible action, emotion, camera framing, recurring {CHARACTER_LOCK} and {PROP_LOCK}, reserved empty bubble space, and a transition to the next panel. No dialogue embedded in images; dialogue lives in {APPROVED_VIETNAMESE_SCRIPT}. Keep panel content culturally specific to {VERIFIED_SETTING} without invented facts about real people or places. Acceptance: coherent reading order and visible change from opening to ending.
```

Vietnamese enters when B approves the script and A letters bubbles/captions **after** visuals are finalized; compare each bubble to the script, proofread accents/punctuation, and zoom final **hosted** rendering at phone size. If generated imagery contains illegible stray lettering, crop/redraw before delivery. [FROM USER] Pricing for images comes from `_common.md`; hosted route/cost stays `UNKNOWN` until checked.

## Judge-lens and cuts

- [HYPOTHESIS] Reward a memorable choice and visual reversal, not panel count. Weak patterns: different face in every panel, long speech balloons, moral tacked onto an unfinished story.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Cut order: extra scene -> secondary character -> background complexity. Keep opening, turn, ending and clear reading direction.
- [HYPOTHESIS] Before contest: prepare character-sheet grid, numbered panel frame, reusable bubble layers and responsive strip reader; rehearse hosting a no-AI dummy comic and verifying fonts/licenses.
